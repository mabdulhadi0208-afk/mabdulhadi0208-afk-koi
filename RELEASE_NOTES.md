## Koi 2.1.2: Cmd+Tab goes to the app you really used last

- **The right app, every time.** Koi now follows the order you actually use apps in (Cmd+Tab, clicking a window, the Dock), so a quick Cmd+Tab always lands on the app you were in just before. 2.1.1 guessed from how windows were stacked, which could pick the wrong app.
- **Works on every setup.** Two displays, an app whose window sits behind another, small helper windows: none of them can take your last app's place any more.
- **Even from an empty desktop.** In an app with no windows open, like Finder, the first Tab goes straight to the app before it.

## Koi 2.1.1: a quick Cmd+Tab switches at once

- **Quick switching works.** Hold Command, tap Tab once and let go: you land straight in the app you used before, with no panel and no picking. In 2.1.0 a quick tap could leave the switcher open instead.
- **The previous app comes first.** The first Tab always goes to the app you used last, even when the app you are in has several windows open. Tab again for the one before that.
- **The panel waits for a hold.** The switcher only appears if you keep holding Command for a moment, like on macOS, so quick taps feel instant with no flash. Fast Tab, Tab, release is never lost.
- **More reliable focus.** If macOS is slow to bring the app forward, Koi asks again, so the switch always lands.
