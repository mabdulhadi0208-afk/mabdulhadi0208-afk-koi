## Koi 2.1: Cmd+Tab, with every window

- **A window switcher with live previews.** Hold Command and press Tab: a glass sheet opens in the middle of the screen with a live thumbnail of every open window, across all your apps, each with its app icon and title. Let go of Command and you land on that exact window, un-minimized if it was tucked away.
- **Replaces Apple's Cmd+Tab.** Koi takes over Command Tab while it runs, so Apple's switcher stays out of the way. If anything goes wrong, Koi hands Command Tab straight back to macOS, so it always works.
- **A rim that glides.** Tab moves forward, Shift Tab moves back, and the arrow keys, hovering, clicking and the scroll wheel all work too. One glowing rim glides from window to window on a soft spring, and the grid scrolls smoothly when you have lots of windows.
- **Most recent first.** The first Tab takes you to the window you used last, just like Windows.
- **Quick actions while you hold Command.** Q quits the app, W closes the window, Esc cancels.
- **Make it yours.** In Hotkeys, Window switcher: turn it on or off, keep Apple's Cmd+Tab (Koi then uses Option Tab), show windows from all apps or just the current Space, and include minimized windows.
- **Clear permissions.** Live previews need Screen Recording; without it, the switcher shows app icons and titles. Taking over Cmd+Tab needs Accessibility, which Koi already asks for. A Koi card shows exactly what to switch on, and no restart is needed.
- **Still zero cost while idle.** Nothing runs while the switcher is closed, and the fan's Command + Option hotkey never fires while you Cmd+Tab.
